# P9-168 Owner Decision Intake Form

## 1. Form status and use boundary

- Work item: `P9-168`
- Activity: docs-only owner decision intake form derived from P9-167
- Source basis: `docs/spec/P9-167_OwnerDecisionPopulationAssistance.md` and `docs/spec/P9-166_OwnerDecisionSubmissionDraft.md`
- Work mode: `docs-only`
- Form status: `COMPLETE / UNPOPULATED / OWNER DECISION REQUIRED / NOT SENT`
- Owner decision state: `OWNER DECISION REQUIRED / NOT RECEIVED`
- Submission state: `NOT AUTHORIZED / NOT SUBMITTED`
- Transmission state: `WITHHOLD / SAFE-STOP`
- Execution-instruction state: `NOT ISSUED`
- Technical execution state: `NO-GO / SAFE-STOP`

This form is for completion by one accountable human owner under that owner's attributable authority. Every uncompleted response remains `OWNER DECISION REQUIRED`. A checked option is not sufficient where the row also requires a basis, value, source, impact, prerequisite, evidence, limitation, or authority statement.

Completing this form does not send or submit it, populate P9-163 or P9-166, accept a package, authorize transmission, issue an execution instruction, resolve the Avast detection, authorize continuation, establish technical GO, or permit technical execution. Codex, a reviewer, or a proxy must not select, infer, recommend, prefill, sign, attest, submit, or transmit an owner-controlled answer.

## 2. Completion instructions

1. Complete section 3 before making the substantive decisions.
2. In section 4, select exactly one option for each of the ten categories and provide every requested detail.
3. In section 5, make a distinct decision for every listed OR ID. Do not use one blanket answer for a classification.
4. In section 6, select exactly one count-discrepancy disposition and complete its required basis.
5. Complete section 7 only after checking the entire form for completeness and consistency.
6. Complete section 8 separately. Form completion and attestation do not imply submission authorization.
7. Do not replace a required answer with a blank, example, `N/A`, or `NONE` unless an authoritative source expressly permits that exact value.

Any omitted, multiple, blanket, partial, contradictory, inferred, unattributable, unauthorized, stale, expired, placeholder-based, unsupported, or unmapped answer retains the whole decision set as `OWNER RESPONSE INCOMPLETE / NOT ACCEPTED / SAFE-STOP`.

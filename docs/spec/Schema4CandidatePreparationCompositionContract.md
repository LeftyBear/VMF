## 2. Candidate Lifecycle

Each preparation attempt SHALL use a fresh UUID as its candidate ID. The candidate ID SHALL be distinct from every future Experiment invocation GUID.

The directory names SHALL be:

```text
vmf-schema4-candidate-{candidate-id:D}.staging
vmf-schema4-candidate-{candidate-id:D}
```

The staging root and final root MUST NOT pre-exist. Candidate preparation SHALL occur entirely within the staging root. Overwrite, merge, and resumption of failed staging are prohibited.

After all validation and readback requirements in this contract succeed, finalization SHALL consist of one same-volume atomic directory rename from the staging root to the final root. No copy-based or cross-volume finalization is permitted.

Failed staging remains **INCOMPLETE / REJECTED**, is never a candidate, and MUST NOT be renamed to the final root. Cleanup or deletion of failed staging requires separate authorization.
